# 09 — GPT-2 124M reproduced from scratch on 2× H100 (val 3.1511, HellaSwag 0.2989)

**Date:** 2026-11-06
**Phase:** 1, item 4 ("Let's reproduce GPT-2 (124M)") + the Phase 1 output item (measured GPT-2 run)
**Full write-up:** `RE/gpt2/WRITEUP.md` (plots, perf table, findings, samples)

Followed Karpathy's "Let's reproduce GPT-2 (124M)" by hand on the Mac (MPS) for the model and training recipe, then moved to rented Nvidia for the speedups, DDP and the full run. Trained GPT-2 124M from scratch on FineWeb-Edu `sample-10BT` (9.95B tokens, 1 epoch, 19,073 steps × 524,288 tokens) on 2× H100 SXM (NVLink), RunPod US-MO-1.

Val loss favours my model by construction (FineWeb-Edu is my training distribution). HellaSwag is the fair comparison. Val loss crossed GPT-2 at step 10,000 (~5.2B tokens); HellaSwag crossed at step 16,500 and was still rising at the end.

## Throughput study (1× H100, one change per row)

| Config | tok/s | bf16 MFU | Gain |
|---|---|---|---|
| FP32, manual attention | 42,275 | — (54% of FP32 peak) | 1.0× |
| + TF32 | 89,709 | — | 2.12× |
| + bf16 | 101,519 | 8.8% | 1.13× |
| + torch.compile | 292,782 | 25.3% | 2.88× |
| + flash attention | 408,158 | 35.3% | 1.39× |
| + vocab 50304 | 434,255 | 37.5% | 1.06× |
| B = 64 | 466,566 | 40.3% | 1.07× (11.0× total) |
| DDP 2 GPUs | 912,982 | 39.4% | 1.96× (98% scaling) |

bf16 barely helped until compile and flash removed the memory-bound attention bottleneck (a 201M-element score matrix per layer, read and written several times). Order matters: each gain depends on the rows before it.

## Predicted vs actual

Predicted training time from the perf session's 912,982 tok/s: 3.04 h. Actual pure step time: 3.20 h. 0.12 h of that was shard-load stalls; without them, 3.08 h (within 1.3%). Evals (78 × ~48 s) were 0.71 h, 18% of wall clock. Predicted run cost ~$33, actual ~$31.

## Where the time goes

- 82% training steps, 18% evals (val + HellaSwag + samples every 250 steps).
- Shard-load stalls: exactly 100 dips, every 190 steps (= 100M tokens/shard ÷ 524,288). `load_tokens()` reads 200 MB from the network volume and builds an 800 MB int64 tensor synchronously on both ranks while the GPUs wait. 7.4 min lost (3.8%). Fix: prefetch the next shard in a background thread, or memory-map and convert per batch.
- Remaining ~60% of peak: many small memory-bound kernels, the optimizer step + clipping, and d=768 matmuls that are small for an H100.

## Findings

1. Shard-load stalls (above).
2. Train-loss discontinuity around step ~5,900; val unaffected → data order, not optimization. Shards are read unshuffled. Fix: shuffle shards and documents.
3. HellaSwag still climbing at the end; 80 tokens/param is already 4× past Chinchilla's ~20, and more training would still help.
4. Evals under `torch.compile`: running HellaSwag and sampling on the uncompiled `raw_model` keeps compiled training at full speed (build-nanogpt skips those evals when compiling).
5. MPS bug: silently wrong loss at B=64 on the Mac (7.14 vs ~10.95). B×T×V = 3.3×10⁹ > 2³¹ elements. B=32 is fine.

## Infra lessons

- Data prep: streaming `datasets` on a 16-vCPU CPU pod, 21 min, $0.17, avoiding a 73 GB HF cache.
- A region-locked network volume made one region's GPU capacity the constraint; the run waited days for 2× H100 in US-MO-1. Cheap-to-regenerate data shouldn't decide where you train.
- `tmux` + `tee` for runs that outlive the SSH session; `2>&1` so tracebacks reach the log.
- `${PIPESTATUS[0]}`, not `$?`, for a command's exit code after a pipe.

## Bugs fixed along the way

ln_1 used for the MLP (should be ln_2); `sd_keys_hf` built from the wrong list; warmup `+` instead of `*`; tokens/sec divided by ms; device checks failing under DDP (fixed with `device_type`); `ReduceOp` typo; uint16 tokens loaded without conversion; HellaSwag URL returning a 404 page (switched to HF `Rowan/hellaswag`); fineweb workers needing a `__main__` guard on macOS.
