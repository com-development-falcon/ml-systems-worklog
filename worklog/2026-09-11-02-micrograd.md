# 02 — micrograd, typed from scratch

**Date:** 2026-09-11
**Phase:** 1, item 1

Built Karpathy's micrograd by hand from the Zero to Hero video — scalar `Value` autograd engine, operator overloads, topological-sort `backward()`, then `Neuron`/`Layer`/`MLP` on top. No copy-paste. Trained a [3 -> 4,4,1] MLP on the toy dataset: loss 3.89 -> 0.06 in 20 steps at lr 0.05, predictions ~[1, -1, -1, 1].

Thing I learned: forgetting to zero the grads doesn't just break training — it *looks better at first*. Stale gradients accumulate into something momentum-like, so early convergence is faster, but the accumulated vector is unbounded and stops pointing at the current loss's gradient. Fast honeymoon, then uncontrollable. Good reminder that "loss went down" is not the same as "correct."
